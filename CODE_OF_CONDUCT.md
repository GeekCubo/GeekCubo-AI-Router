# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in GeekCubo to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.
- Showing empathy and patience towards other contributors, especially newcomers.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome advances.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behaviour.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions, reviews) and when someone officially represents the project in
public spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They
may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily
or permanently restrict anyone whose behaviour they deem inappropriate.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through GitHub's
private vulnerability reporting form
(<https://github.com/GeekCubo/GeekCubo-AI-Router/security/advisories/new>),
starting the title with "Code of Conduct". Reports are reviewed promptly and
handled confidentially.
